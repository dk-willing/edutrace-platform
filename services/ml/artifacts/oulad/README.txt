EduTrace model trained on REAL data: OULAD (Open University Learning
Analytics Dataset), 32,593 learners, CC-BY 4.0.

WHAT THIS VALIDATES
  The methodology. Discrete-time hazard labelling, administrative censoring,
  strictly temporal splits, isotonic calibration, capacity-based metrics and
  the fairness gates all ran end to end on a real population with a real,
  dated leaving event.

WHAT IT DOES NOT VALIDATE
  Anything about Ghanaian junior high school learners. OULAD is UK adults
  studying at a distance; "attendance" here is a click-based proxy; fees,
  distance to school, child labour, guardian structure and behaviour records
  do not exist in it at all.

DO NOT use this model to score a school learner. It is a methodology
validation artifact. Train on school register data before scoring children.
